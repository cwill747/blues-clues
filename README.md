# 🐾 Blue's Clues at Home

A playful, parent-led scavenger hunt inspired by the classic three-clue mystery game. Turn everyday household objects into clues and help little detectives solve 100 mysteries.

**[Play the game](https://cwill747.github.io/blues-clues/)**

## Printable paw prints

**[Download the one-page PDF with three blue paw prints](./paw-prints.pdf)**. Print it on US Letter paper, cut out the three paw prints, and place them on clue objects around the house. Reuse the prints for every mystery. The same PDF is linked from the in-app **How to play** screen.

## How it works

1. **Choose a mystery.** Each mystery has a question, three objects to use as clues, and a secret answer.
2. **Set up the hunt.** A grown-up draws or cuts out three blue paw prints, puts one on each clue object, and hides them in different spots around the house. A quick sketch can stand in for an object you don't have.
3. **Start playing.** Tap **Done hiding** to switch from parent setup to child-friendly play mode.
4. **Find the clues.** Search the house together and tap each clue in the app when discovered.
5. **Think it through.** Once all three clues are found, visit the thinking chair and tap **Solve the mystery!** to reveal the answer.
6. **Celebrate.** Tap **Finish & next mystery** to save completion and advance in one step.

A short, three-step **How to play** introduction appears on first launch. Tap **ⓘ How to play** at any time to see it again. Installation instructions are available in its expandable **Add this game to your phone** section.

## Features

- **100 ready-to-play mysteries**, with varied questions and clue-specific hints, built around common toys, household objects, pretend play, snacks, and activities.
- **Parent setup mode** shows the objects and secret answer before the hunt.
- **Child-friendly play mode** hides the clues until they're discovered.
- **Completion tracking** shows how many mysteries you've finished; **Finish & next mystery** records completion and advances automatically.
- **Random mystery** prioritizes unfinished games.
- **Saved progress** via browser `localStorage` on the current device.
- **Installable PWA** with standalone display and a home-screen icon.
- **Offline support** after the app has loaded and cached successfully.
- **No accounts, advertisements, backend, or analytics.**

## Install on your phone

The game can be added to your Home Screen like an app. Once it has loaded and cached successfully, it can also work offline.

### iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open [Blue's Clues at Home](https://cwill747.github.io/blues-clues/) in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and select **Add to Home Screen**. Depending on your iOS version, you may need to open **More**.
4. Tap **Add**. If an **Open as Web App** option appears, keep it enabled.
5. Tap the **Blue's Clues** icon on your Home Screen to play.

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open [Blue's Clues at Home](https://cwill747.github.io/blues-clues/) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu next to the address bar.
3. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.
4. Launch the game from the new Home Screen icon.

You can find these instructions inside the app at any time by tapping **ⓘ How to play**.

## Privacy and storage

Completed mysteries and whether the introduction has been dismissed are stored only in the browser's local storage. Progress does not sync across devices. Clearing website data or removing the installed app may erase saved progress.

## Technology

A dependency-free static website using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. A web app manifest enables installation, and a service worker caches the application for offline use. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
