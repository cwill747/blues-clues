Ready to play Blue’s Clues?

A little mystery, right in your home!

🐾 1. Hide three clues. A grown-up puts a blue paw print on each object, in different spots around the house.

🔎 2. Find the clues. Your little detective searches for all three!

💡 3. Solve the mystery! Sit in the thinking chair, put the clues together, and guess.

🖨️ Print 3 blue paw prints (PDF)

One page. Print, cut out, and reuse for each mystery.

📱 Add this game to your phone

Install on your Home Screen for quick access and offline play after the first load.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (square with an upward arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add. Keep Open as Web App on if offered.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.

Your progress stays on this device. Reopen this guide anytime using ⓘ How to play.
