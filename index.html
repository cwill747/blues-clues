Let’s play Blue’s Clues!

Turn everyday toys and household objects into a fun mystery for your little detective.

  1. Pick a mystery. A grown-up reads the question and the three clues.
  2. Hide the clues. Draw or cut out three blue paw prints and place one on each object. Use a drawing if you don’t have an item.
  3. Tap “Done hiding”. The answers disappear, and it’s time to search!
  4. Find all three clues. Tap each paw print in the app as your child discovers it. Shout “A clue! A clue!”
  5. Visit the thinking chair. Talk about how the clues fit together, then reveal the answer.
  6. Mark it done. Save your progress and choose another mystery. Random mysteries favor ones you haven’t finished.

💡 Your completed mysteries are saved on this device. The app works offline after its first successful load. You can open these instructions again anytime with ⓘ How to play.

📱 Add this game to your phone

Install it on your Home Screen for quick access, an app-like experience, and offline play after the first load.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button □↑ (usually at the bottom of the screen).
  3. Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen. On newer iOS versions, you may find it under More.
  4. Choose Add. If offered Open as Web App, leave it enabled.
  5. Launch Blue's Clues from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu near the address bar.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
  4. Open the game from your Home Screen.

Already installed? You're all set. Your completed mysteries are saved on this device, not synced between devices.
